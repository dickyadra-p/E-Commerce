Common concerns

What Stands Between You and the Next Shipment

How long does store setup take?
A basic store with products, payment, and shipping rules can be operational in under an hour. Importing an existing catalog from CSV takes minutes. Custom fulfillment rules and API integrations typically require one to three business days, depending on complexity.
What happens if inventory sync fails mid-order?
The platform holds the order in a verified pending state and surfaces an exception immediately. No charge is captured until inventory is confirmed. You see the issue before the customer experiences a backorder.
Can I use my existing warehouse system?
Yes. The Scale plan includes a full API for connecting existing WMS, ERP, or 3PL systems. Business plans support CSV-based batch exports. Real-time webhook integrations are available on all plans for order status updates.
What makes this different from a marketplace like Shopify or WooCommerce?
Those platforms excel at storefront creation. E-Commerce focuses on what happens after the buy button: inventory propagation, fulfillment routing, and exception management. Many merchants run both — a storefront on their preferred platform and order operations here.
